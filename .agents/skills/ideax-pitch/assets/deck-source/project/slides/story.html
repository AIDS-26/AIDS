0:15–0:45. Tell it as a story, slowly, looking at the judges, not the slide: “Picture a mall’s CCTV room. Sensors raise an alarm every few seconds, and most alarms are a door swinging open. After a week the guards stop looking. Then a real break-in rings an alarm, and nobody checks it.” Point at the red tile on “nobody checks it”. Then: “Company security teams live in that room. Industry surveys put the average at about 3,000 security alerts a day, with most of them never read. The sensors already see the attacks. The problem is that people cannot read everything the sensors raise.” Click to the next slide on “3,000” so the number appears as you say it.
